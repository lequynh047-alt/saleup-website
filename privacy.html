Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

SALEUP PTY LTD (ABN 74 642 236 511) ("SALEUP", "we", "us") provides the ChillBoss software and this website. We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

Who this policy covers

Business owners and staff who use ChillBoss ("our customers"), and the people who contact or are contacted by those businesses through ChillBoss ("end customers"), such as callers, people who book appointments or check in, and people who receive messages.

What we collect

  • Account details of our customers: name, business name, email, phone, billing details (card payments are processed by Stripe; we don't store full card numbers).
  • Information our customers store about their end customers: names, phone numbers, email addresses, appointment history, notes, optional birthday (day and month only), loyalty visits, review ratings and feedback.
  • Call and message content: call recordings, transcripts and summaries handled by the AI receptionist, and SMS/email messages sent or received through ChillBoss.
  • Technical information: device and browser data, IP address and usage logs, used to keep the service secure and working.

How we use it

  • To provide ChillBoss: answering calls, booking appointments, sending confirmations and reminders, check-ins, loyalty rewards and review requests.
  • To send marketing messages on behalf of our customers only where the end customer has consented, with an unsubscribe option in every message.
  • To support, secure and improve the service, and to meet our legal obligations.

We never sell personal information.

Our customers' responsibilities

For information about their end customers, our customers decide what is collected and why. SALEUP processes it on their behalf. If you're an end customer of a business that uses ChillBoss, you can contact that business, or us, about your information.

AI and service providers

ChillBoss uses trusted providers to run, including cloud hosting and databases (our main database is in Sydney, Australia), telephony and SMS (Twilio), email delivery, payments (Stripe) and AI models (such as OpenAI) to understand and respond to calls and messages. Some of these providers may process information outside Australia, including in the United States. We take reasonable steps to ensure they protect it in a way consistent with the APPs.

Calls answered by the ChillBoss AI receptionist identify themselves as an AI and may be recorded and transcribed so the business can follow up.

Security and retention

We use encryption in transit, access controls and logging to protect information, and keep it only as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal requirements. Our customers can delete their end customers' records from the app.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask to access or correct your personal information, or make a privacy complaint, by emailing hello@saleup.com.au. We aim to respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.
