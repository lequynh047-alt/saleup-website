SMS & messaging policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains how SMS messages are sent through ChillBoss, a service of SALEUP PTY LTD (ABN 74 642 236 511), on behalf of the businesses that use it.

Who sends the messages

Messages are sent by or on behalf of the local business you dealt with (for example a salon or clinic), using ChillBoss. Each message identifies the business.

Types of messages

  • Service messages: booking confirmations, reminders, check-in confirmations, and a short "how was your visit?" message after an appointment.
  • Marketing messages: offers, birthday gifts and news from the business. These are only sent if you agreed to receive them — for example by ticking the opt-in box when booking or checking in. The box is never ticked for you.

How often

Service messages relate to your appointments. Marketing messages vary by business and are typically no more than a few per month. Review requests are sent at most once every 90 days.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving messages from that business. You'll receive one confirmation and nothing further.
  • Reply HELP or email hello@saleup.com.au for help.
  • Every marketing message includes opt-out instructions, in line with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

Costs

We don't charge you to receive messages. Standard carrier rates may apply to replies, depending on your mobile plan.

Your information

Phone numbers and consent records are used only to send messages for the business you dealt with and are never sold or shared for others' marketing. See our privacy policy.
